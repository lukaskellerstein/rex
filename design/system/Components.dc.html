ANCHOR STATE — ONE VOCABULARY, FOUR PLACES The card carries its own state. Its body takes a low-saturation wash of the state colour with a matching border, and the numbered token repeats that colour at full strength. Selection deepens the same wash and brightens the same border — a card never changes hue when you select it, only intensity, which is what keeps state and selection legible on one surface. State is named in words in the meta line too, so nothing rests on colour alone.

WHAT THE DOCUMENT PANE MAY AND MAY NOT RESTYLE The 620px measure and the 15/1.68 setting apply only to markdown REX renders itself — that stylesheet is REX's own. Sanitised author HTML keeps its own styles, and a <webview> URL is untouchable. For those two the pane supplies only the paper ground and the gutter, and the design must survive the document looking like anything at all.
STILL UNDECIDED Bundling Plex Sans and Newsreader adds roughly 400KB to the app — worth confirming before it goes in. The empty state, the truncated-tree warning, the URL-open path and the synthesis-thread builder are not drawn yet; they inherit these tokens but need their own screens.
