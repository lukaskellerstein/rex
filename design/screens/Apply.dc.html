Widget service notes
The widget service accepts requests on a queue and writes results to the database. Each request is acknowledged before the work begins.
Retries
A failed write is retried three times with exponential backoff, starting at 200ms and doubling, then routed to the dead-letter queue. Every drop is recorded there with the original payload and the last error.
The backoff exists to avoid a thundering herd against a dependency that is already failing. The retry budget is three in both this document and the components table.
