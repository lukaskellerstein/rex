PICKING THINGS THAT ARE NOT TEXT
Getting to the figure By hovering it in pick mode — the toolbar button, E, or holding ⌥. See the Hovering to pick board for the outline, the badge and the path bar. This board picks up once the figure is the target.
Region drag Once a figure is picked, dragging inside it stores a box as fractions of the element — createRegionAnchor(), already written. Useful for “this bar”, “this quadrant”, “this row of the diagram”.
WHAT THIS COSTS TO BUILD Two methods on DocumentSurface — anchorFromElement() and anchorFromRegion() — plus their two implementations and the hover/drag layer inside the frame. The anchor creation, resolution and storage all exist.
PDF AND DOCX — BLOCKED, NOT DESIGNED AWAY You cannot select in a document REX cannot open. render/index.ts renders Markdown and HTML only; unopenableReason() calls PDF and DOCX “tier 3, not scheduled”, and the explorer greys them out. Everything on this page works on them the day a tier‑3 renderer produces a DOM — the anchor kinds do not care where the DOM came from. That renderer is the decision, and it is not mine to take.
